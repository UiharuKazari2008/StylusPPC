WMDroid Skin Designer

Selected

Tap an element on the phone. Shift-click to select more. Drag to move, drag the blue corner to resize, arrow keys nudge (Shift = 10×).

Align

Moves the selection on the phone. Align lines items up with each other. Space needs three or more.

Order

Later elements paint in front. Front and Back move the selection to the ends. Up and Down move it one step.

Export / import

Positions are fractions of the canvas, so the layout scales to any phone. A Dock rectangle is where the Android app dock is drawn. Send the JSON (or skin.json) back and it becomes the skin.
